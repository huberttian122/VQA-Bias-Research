<h1 align = "center">🤖VQA Bias Research</h1>

**This repository curates research on biases and debiasing approaches in Visual Question Answering (VQA). It is organized around our survey, _Biases and Debiasing Approaches in Visual Question Answering: A Comprehensive Survey_, which reviews 423 publications on language, visual, distributional, and sociocultural biases, together with data-centric, model-centric, learning-centric, and LLM-era debiasing methods.**
